# Brand Direction Decision

## Chosen Direction: Ledger Lines (Direction 1)

**Rationale:** The three horizontal rules of descending length directly evoke a teacher's mark book — the core mental model of the product. The lowest line in the accent colour marks the C+ pass threshold, making the grading logic visible in the mark itself. This direction is uniquely legible at 16px (three distinct strokes), reproduces perfectly in single-colour print, and scales without optical distortion because it contains no curves or diagonal strokes to anti-alias poorly. The "C" shape formed by the left-aligned rules reads as both the initial of Chokur and an open ledger waiting to be filled.
